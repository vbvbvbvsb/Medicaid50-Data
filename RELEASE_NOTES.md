First public release of Medicaid50 Data under ODbL 1.0.

- 51 jurisdictions: all 50 states and DC.
- 60 managed-care route/contact records, 436 sources and 21 portals.
- 358 practitioner answer sections and 230 open practitioner questions.
- Full JSON, schema and 10 freshly generated CSV exports.
- Provenance notes, citation metadata, validation utilities and SHA-256 checksums.

Packaged November 16, 2026. Per-fact verification dates and source/service-date limits remain authoritative; packaging did not reverify every external source. Practitioner confirmations remain attributed. Known contact routes do not establish completed case handling or universal plan clearance.

The database is licensed under [ODbL 1.0](https://opendatacommons.org/licenses/odbl/1-0/). Utility code is MIT licensed; third-party source contents retain their own rights. Private application history, runtime records, correspondence, practice attachments and research captures are excluded.
